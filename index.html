How this works

You said you kind of get FBDs and energy charts, but you don't know how to get the equations. Everything here is built around that one skill:

Draw the picture → every arrow/bar becomes one term → solve in symbols → plug in numbers last.

  1. Basics: the base knowledge drilled until it's automatic: must-know facts (free fall, when N = mg, when energy is conserved…), "which principle do I use?", true/false, an FBD builder, and an energy bar chart builder.
  2. Learn (no problems): short video + recipe + fully worked example for each concept, plus how to do lab and graph FRQs.
  3. Practice (endless, new numbers): Guided mode walks you through the FBD/bar chart → equation → number, and every wrong choice tells you why. Then Quick mode, then ★ Hard.
  4. FRQs: 10 long AP/MIT-style problems with lab data, graphs, and derivations, graded with a point rubric.
  5. Exam Sim: 90 minutes like the real test: 12 short + 2 FRQs, timed, no going back.
  6. Mistakes: everything you miss, until you get it right.
