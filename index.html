How this works

You said you kind of get FBDs and energy charts, but you don't know how to get the equations. Everything here is built around that one skill:

Draw the picture → every arrow/bar becomes one term → solve in symbols → plug in numbers last.

  1. Learn (no problems): short video + the recipe + one fully worked example per concept. Copy the purple recipe boxes into your notebook.
  2. Practice (endless, new numbers every time): Guided mode walks you through each problem: pick the forces for the FBD, pick the right equation, then type the number. Every wrong choice tells you why it's wrong. Switch to Quick mode once you can do it alone.
  3. Timed test: 19 mixed questions, a timer, lock-in with no going back, then a full key with the work.
  4. Mistakes: everything you miss is saved here so you can redo it until you get it right.
